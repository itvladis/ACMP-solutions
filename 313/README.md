# Minute buses

Every minute a bus of some route arrives at a stop, and the route numbers for N minutes
are recorded. Find the longest possible wait for a passenger who needs one particular
route, i.e. the largest distance between two equal numbers in the sequence that have no
equal number between them. Every route appears at least twice.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N (2 ≤ N ≤ 10^6), then N route numbers (natural numbers up to 100).
**Output** (`output.txt`): the maximum waiting time.

Examples: `2 11 2 2 25 11 25 11` → `4`, `23 23 41 41` → `1`.
