# Dunno

A starship flies forward S1 km during T1 hours, then must recharge for T2 hours, drifting
back S2 km during that time, and the cycle repeats. Movement is uniform within each phase.
Determine how long it takes to reach a planet at distance S.

**Input** (`input.txt`): five natural numbers T1, T2, S1, S2, S, each at most 10^7.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the time in hours with two digits after the decimal point, or `NO` if the planet is unreachable.

Examples: `5 3 5 3 4` → `4.00`, `5 3 5 3 6` → `12.00`, `5 1 3 3 6` → `NO`, `10 1 100 20 500` → `65.00`.
