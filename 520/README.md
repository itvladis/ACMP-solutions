# Wholesale purchase

A pair of socks costs 10.50, a bundle of 12 pairs costs 102.50 and a box of 12 bundles costs
1140. A customer needs N pairs. Find how many boxes, bundles and single pairs to buy so that at
least N pairs are bought for the lowest total price.

**Input** (`input.txt`): a natural number N (N ≤ 10^9).
**Output** (`output.txt`): three numbers: boxes, bundles and pairs, separated by spaces.

Examples: `11` → `0 1 0`, `500` → `3 5 8`.
