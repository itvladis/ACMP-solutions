# Hairy business

A man decides to make a living by selling his hair. On day 0 his hair is 1 cm long, and
it grows by exactly 1 cm every night. On each of the next N days (numbered 0 to N-1) the
price of one centimeter of hair is known in advance; on any day he may cut and sell all
the hair he currently has. Find the maximum amount of money he can earn over the N days.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N (1 ≤ N ≤ 100), then N natural numbers up to 100 — the price per centimeter on each day.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the maximum total earnings.

Examples: `5 / 73 31 96 24 46` → `380`, `10 / 1 2 ... 10` → `100`, `10 / 10 9 ... 1` → `55`.
