# Birthday - 2

Given the day and month of a birthday and today's date, find how many days are left until the
next birthday (0 if it is today). A person born on February 29 celebrates only in leap years.

**Input** (`input.txt`): the birthday day and month on the first line, today's day, month and year (1920–3000) on the second.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the number of days until the birthday.

Examples: `19 04` / `19 04 2002` → `0`, `05 05` / `19 04 2002` → `16`, `29 02` / `28 02 2001` → `1096`.
