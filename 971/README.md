# Triangle 4

The center of mass of triangle ABC is the point whose coordinates are the averages of the
vertex coordinates. Given the center of mass, find a non-degenerate triangle with integer vertex
coordinates not exceeding 10^9 in absolute value, or report that it does not exist.

**Input** (`input.txt`): two integers x and y (−10^9 ≤ x, y ≤ 10^9), the center of mass.
**Output** (`output.txt`): `YES` and three lines with the vertex coordinates (any valid answer), or `NO`.

Examples: `9 4` → `YES` / `3 9` / `6 3` / `18 0`.
