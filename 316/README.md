# Telegraph transfer

A money transfer costs 7 rubles for every full or partial hundred of the sent amount. Someone
has N rubles and the fee is paid from the same money. Find the largest amount that can be sent
and the fee for it.

**Input** (`input.txt`): a natural number N (8 ≤ N ≤ 6·10^4).
**Output** (`output.txt`): the maximum amount and the transfer fee, separated by a space.

Examples: `10` → `3 7`, `108` → `100 7`.
