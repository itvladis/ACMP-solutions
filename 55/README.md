# Flashlights

A flashlight with two bulbs lights two circles of equal radius r on a wall. Given the
circle centers, r and the area s lit by an old one-bulb flashlight, decide whether the new
flashlight lights a larger area (the area of the union of the two circles).

**Input** (`input.txt`): x1 y1, x2 y2, r and s on separate lines (integers, coordinates
and r from 1 to 100, 1 ≤ s ≤ 10^5).
**Output** (`output.txt`): `YES` if the new flashlight is better, otherwise `NO`.

Examples: `1 2`, `3 4`, `2`, `22` → `YES`; `1 1`, `100 100`, `1`, `7` → `NO`.
