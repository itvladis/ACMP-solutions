# Queen, rook and knight

A queen, a rook and a knight stand on an 8x8 chessboard. Count the empty squares that are
attacked by at least one of them. For simplicity, pieces are allowed to attack through other
pieces.

**Input** (`input.txt`): the positions of the queen, the rook and the knight, each as a letter `A`-`H` followed by a digit `1`-`8`.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the number of empty squares under attack.

Examples: `D1 D3 E5` → `29`.
