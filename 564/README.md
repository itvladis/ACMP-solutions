# Fence - 2

A warehouse holds n straight fence fragments of given lengths. The director wants to
pick three fragments and fence off a triangular plot of the largest possible area.
Find that maximal area and the indices of the chosen fragments, or report that no plot
of non-zero area can be built.

**Input** (`input.txt`): the number n (n ≤ 100), then n positive integers not exceeding 1000 — the lengths.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the maximal area (with at least 6 digits after the decimal point) and, on the next
line, the indices of the three fragments in any order; or `-1` if a non-degenerate
triangle is impossible.

Examples: `3` / `1 1 1` → `0.4330127019` / `1 2 3`, `4` / `1 2 4 7` → `-1`.
