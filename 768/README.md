# Star rectangles

Given N stars on a plane, count how many different sets of four stars form the vertices
of a rectangle.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N (1 ≤ N ≤ 100), then N pairs of integer coordinates.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the number of rectangles.

Examples: `4 / 1 1 / 10 10 / 1 10 / 10 1` → `1`, `4 / 1 1 / 2 10 / 9 10 / 10 1` → `0`.
