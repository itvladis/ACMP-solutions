# Bonus

Bonuses in a company list are written in different bases. The base for an employee is the last
digit of his age plus 2. Given the age and the bonus written in that base (digits and capital
letters), convert the bonus to decimal. The bonus never exceeds 100000.

**Input** (`input.txt`): two values: the age N (at most 100) and the bonus K in its base.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the bonus in decimal.

Examples: `28 2800` → `2800`, `30 101` → `5`.
