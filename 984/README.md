# ACM ICPC finals

At most N teams advance to the finals, with no more than K teams from one university,
choosing the set with the smallest sum of places. Given the semifinal standings (the
university of each place and the team numbers), list the advancing teams in order of place.

**Input** (`input.txt`): P, N, K (N, K ≤ P ≤ 10^5), then P lines with university names in order of place, then a line with the P team numbers.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the advancing teams as `University #ID`, one per line.

Examples: `9 5 2` with standings Fantasy, Crazy, Fantasy, Fantasy, Very Good U, Good U, Very Good U, Crazy, Good U and numbers `1 1 2 3 2 1 1 2 2` → `Fantasy University #1` / `Crazy University #1` / `Fantasy University #2` / `Very Good U #2` / `Good U #1`.
