# Wedding

A scheming woman wants to get rich by marrying and immediately divorcing some of N
admirers. On divorce, the combined property of the couple is split equally between the
two spouses. Each man may be used at most once, in any order, and she must end up
unmarried. Find the largest amount of money she can end up with.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N (1 < N ≤ 40), then N non-negative integers — the men's savings, then her own savings A (all up to 10^9).
**Output** (`output.txt`): the maximum final amount with exactly six digits after the decimal point.

Examples: `2 / 5 10 / 5` → `7.500000`, `3 / 1 3 2 / 0` → `2.125000`.
