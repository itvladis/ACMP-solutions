# Rectangles

Count the non-degenerate axis-parallel rectangles whose vertices are integer points
lying inside or on the border of the rectangle with opposite corners (0, 0) and (W, H).

**Input** (`input.txt`): two natural numbers W and H, not exceeding 1000.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the number of such rectangles.

Examples: `1 1` → `1`, `2 1` → `3`, `2 2` → `9`.
