# Queen and king

On a chessboard there are a white king, a white queen and a black king. Determine whether the
black king is in check, i.e. whether the queen can reach its square in one move along a rank,
file or diagonal without jumping over the white king.

**Input** (`input.txt`): three squares in chess notation: white king, white queen, black king.
**Output** (`output.txt`): `YES` if the black king is in check, otherwise `NO`.

Examples: `d3 d5 d8` → `YES`, `a1 a8 b3` → `NO`.
