# Approaching the target

A target starts at (x0, y0) and moves uniformly with velocity (Vx, Vy). A cruiser starts at
the origin, can move with speed at most V and change direction instantly. Determine whether
after exactly t units of time the cruiser can be at distance exactly d from the target.

**Input** (`input.txt`): x0 y0, then Vx Vy, then V t d (|x0|, |y0| ≤ 10⁹, |Vx|, |Vy| ≤ 10⁶, 1 ≤ V ≤ 10⁶, 1 ≤ t ≤ 1000, 1 ≤ d ≤ 10⁹).
**Output** (`output.txt`): `YES` or `NO`.

Examples: `1 1 / 1 1 / 1 1 1` → `NO`, `1 1 / 1 1 / 1 1 2` → `YES`.
