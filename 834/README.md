# Estate

A knight was granted a circular piece of land around a castle, together with a straight road
from the place of the conversation to the castle. Since the king only granted convex territories,
the knight actually owns the convex hull of the circle and that point. Find its area.

**Input** (`input.txt`): the point (xk, yk) on the first line, the center (xc, yc) and radius rc on the second; all values are reals with absolute value at most 10^4.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the area with at least three digits after the decimal point.

Examples: `2 5 / 2 1 1` → `5.69646`.
