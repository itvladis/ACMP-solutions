# Set intersection

Two unordered collections of integers (possibly with repeats) are given. Print, in increasing order
and without duplicates, every number that occurs in both collections.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N and M (1 ≤ N, M ≤ 300000), then N numbers, then M numbers, each from 0 to 10^5.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the common numbers in increasing order separated by spaces; empty output if there are none.

Examples: `11 6` / `2 4 6 8 10 12 10 8 6 4 2` / `3 6 9 12 15 18` → `6 12`.
