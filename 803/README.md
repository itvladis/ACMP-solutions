# Digits after the decimal point

Given positive integers A and B, find the K-th digit after the decimal point of the fraction
A/B written in decimal.

**Input** (`input.txt`): three numbers A, B and K, where A and B are digits from 1 to 9 and K ≤ 10^6.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the K-th digit after the decimal point.

Examples: `7 4 2` → `5`, `1 6 13` → `6`.
