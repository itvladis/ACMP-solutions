# Gold of the Abba tribe

The chief may choose one of three piles of gold coins and wants the largest one. Given the sizes
of the piles, output the maximum.

**Input** (`input.txt`): three natural numbers, each up to 10^100, without leading zeros.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the largest of them.

Examples: `5 7 3` → `7`, `987531 234 86364` → `987531`.
