# Buses - 2

N children and M adults are going to a camp, and every bus holds K people. Any bus that carries
at least one child must also carry at least two adults. Find the minimum number of buses needed
to take everyone, or report that it is impossible.

**Input** (`input.txt`): three natural numbers N, M and K (each up to 10 000).
**Output** (`output.txt`): the minimum number of buses, or 0 if everyone can't be transported.

Examples: `10 4 7` → `2`, `10 4 5` → `0`.
